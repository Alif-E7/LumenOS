# LumenOS - Demo Script

**Total Duration: 3 Minutes**

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] THE HOOK

**Visual/Action:** Start on a blank screen or a simple title slide: "LumenOS: The Next Frontier". Look directly at the judges.

**Speaker:**
"Judges, in 2026, companies are spending billions to put AI data centers in space. But there's one problem nobody talks about. In the vacuum of space, there's no air. No fans. No convection. Your $2 billion AI server will melt in 14 minutes."

---

### [0:30 - 1:00] THE PROBLEM

**Visual/Action:** Switch to the dashboard view. Point to the **Naive Scheduler** metric card and its Thermal Timeline.

**Speaker:**
"Here is what happens when you use a standard data center scheduler in orbit. It’s thermally blind. Look at the red temperature line. As the satellite flies into the sunlit Earth-facing zone, the radiator loses cooling efficiency. The temperature skyrockets, hitting 95°C. And right there—SHUTDOWN. The server crashes, data is lost, and the hardware degrades."

---

### [1:00 - 2:00] THE SOLUTION

**Visual/Action:** Gesture to the **LumenOS** metrics card, the 3D Globe, and the blue temperature line in the timeline. Highlight the four modules conceptually.

**Speaker:**
"Enter LumenOS. We built an operating system powered by four modules: an Orbital Engine, a Thermal Engine, a Workload Profiler, and the Lumen Scheduler. 

Instead of waiting for a crash, LumenOS calculates orbital mechanics and thermodynamics in real time. Notice the blue temperature line? It stays perfectly safe. Because LumenOS looks 5 minutes into the future, it knows when the radiator is about to face Earth. It gracefully pauses heavy AI tasks, saving progress, and runs low-heat tasks until the satellite reaches deep space again. We prevent shutdowns before they happen."

---

### [2:00 - 2:30] THE IMPACT

**Visual/Action:** Highlight the **+39% Productivity Gain** card on the dashboard.

**Speaker:**
"Sophia Space is building this for $100 million. We built an open-source proof-of-concept in 48 hours. By maximizing useful compute time and preventing thermal damage, we achieved a massive 39% productivity gain over the baseline. This is the operating system that makes the Next Frontier physically possible."

---

### [2:30 - 3:00] CLOSING

**Visual/Action:** Final confident look at the judges. (Optional: Leave the dashboard on screen so they can see the interactive globe spinning).

**Speaker:**
"We didn't just build an app. We taught software to feel the temperature of the cosmos. Thank you."
