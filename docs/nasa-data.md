# NASA & Partner Data Integration, Provenance & Offline Fallback

LumenOS integrates empirical space agency observations to anchor educational mission simulations in real-world science. To maintain absolute scientific transparency, real data is strictly isolated from simulated spacecraft sensor observations and ground truth.

---

## 1. Four-Tier Semantic Data Provenance Framework

Every telemetry item, map overlay, and event notification is tagged with one of four immutable provenance tags:

| Category | UI Badge | Authority Level | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| `REAL_NASA_REFERENCE` | **REAL NASA / REFERENCE DATA** | Authoritative Empirical | Real-world published observations from NASA, NOAA, ESA, or CelesTrak archives. Used for environment calibration and mission context. |
| `SIMULATED_SPACECRAFT` | **SIMULATED SPACECRAFT OBSERVATION** | Simulated Sensor Output | Telemetry and payload data captured by the player's onboard instruments (subject to sensor noise, smoke obscuration, and downlink limits). |
| `SIMULATED_ENVIRONMENT` | **SIMULATED ENVIRONMENT / GROUND TRUTH** | Authoritative World State | Background ground truth maintained by the simulation engine (e.g. true ground fire size, true orbit vector, eclipse entry). |
| `MODEL_PREDICTION` | **MODEL OR AI PREDICTION** | Estimated / Uncertain | Forward forecasts generated by the LumenOS predictive scheduler (subject to forecast horizon and estimation uncertainty). |

---

## 2. Explicit NASA FIRMS Operational Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NASA FIRMS Active Fire Detections Disclaimer**:
> NASA FIRMS active fire pixels are real-world empirical observations acquired by the VIIRS instrument on the Suomi NPP and NOAA-20 polar orbiters. They are **NOT** images captured by the fictional LumenOS spacecraft, and they do not represent infallible ground truth. In Wildfire Scout, FIRMS data serves as an external operational reference to validate satellite detections.
